About Me

I Don't Just Install Jira — I Architect It for Your Goals

With over a decade in IT Project Management, I have lived inside Jira — using it daily to track, monitor, and control complex projects across teams of all sizes. I know exactly where the pain points are because I have dealt with them myself.

Now I bring that same operational expertise to freelance clients: from early-stage startups setting up their first sprint board, to multi-team programs needing a cross-functional Jira ecosystem that leaders, QA, and Dev can all trust.
